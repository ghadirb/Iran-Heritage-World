## آنچه کودک یاد می‌گیرد
رخش اسب افسانه‌ای رستم است. در شاهنامه رستم از میان اسب‌های گله دست بر پشتشان می‌گذارد و همه خم می‌شوند جز کره‌ای به رنگ برگ‌های سرخ گل بر زمینه‌ی زعفرانی؛ او را با کمند می‌گیرد و رخش می‌نامد.

## Sources (accessed 17 Nov 2026; a human reviewer must re-check each claim)
- Primary (lines NOT yet recorded): Ferdowsi, *Shahnameh*, "Rostam chooses Rakhsh" (the episode before Rostam's first battles). Edition/lines must be added by a human reviewer.
- Smithsonian Freer|Sackler, "Rustam & Rakhsh" (Shahnama learning resource): the colt "flecked as with petals of red roses on saffron"; Rustam subdues him by throwing a lasso around his neck.
- FEZANA Lesson 7, "Rostam and Rakhsh" (grades 4–5): Rostam presses his hands on the horses' backs and each buckles; at last a herd from Kabul, a gray mare and her foal; lasso; the foal barely feels the pressure; "You are the horse for me".
- Wikipedia "Rakhsh": colour "rose leaves scattered upon a saffron ground"; faithful steed of Rostam.
- Metropolitan Museum object note on Rustam capturing Rakhsh with his lasso.
